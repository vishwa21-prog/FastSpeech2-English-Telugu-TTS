# FastSpeech 2 — English and Telugu Results

This directory organizes audio samples and diagnostic visualizations from the English–Telugu text-to-speech experiments.

## Directory structure

```text
results/
├── README.md
├── FastSpeech2_Analysis_Dashboard.png
├── English/
│   ├── training_loss.png
│   ├── validation_loss.png
│   ├── ground_truth_reconstruction.wav
│   ├── test_generated_audios/
│   ├── pretrained_baseline/
│   ├── calibrated_audio/
│   └── variance_adaptor_plots/
└── Telugu/
    ├── training_loss.png
    ├── validation_loss.png
    ├── ground_truth_reconstruction.wav
    ├── test_generated_audios/
    ├── pretrained_baseline/
    ├── calibrated_audio/
    └── variance_adaptor_plots/
```

## What's included

- **Training and validation loss plots:** Track model optimization and validation behavior separately for English and Telugu.
- **Test-generated audio:** WAV samples collected during the FastSpeech 2 experimental pipeline, organized by language.
- **Pretrained baseline:** Reference speech samples from a pretrained MMS-VITS TTS model for qualitative comparison; **these are not generated by the trained FastSpeech 2 models**.
- **Calibrated audio:** Audio samples from the calibration experiments, kept separate from baseline and other generated samples.
- **Ground-truth reconstruction:** Reconstructed reference audio for listening comparison.
- **Variance adaptor plots:** Per-test visualizations of predicted or analyzed **duration, pitch, and energy**, the prosodic features modeled by FastSpeech 2.
- **Analysis dashboard:** A combined visual overview of experimental results.

For each language, matching filenames such as `test_01.wav` across audio subfolders identify corresponding test cases. The variance adaptor plots follow the pattern `duration_test_01.png`, `pitch_test_01.png`, and `energy_test_01.png`.

## Interpretation

Audio samples and plots document experiments and provide qualitative or diagnostic evidence. They should not be treated alone as proof of production-quality speech synthesis or model accuracy. Compare experimental outputs with the pretrained baseline and reference reconstructions, and use the loss plots to understand training behavior.

The results are organized to keep the **English and Telugu** experimental artifacts clearly separated and easy to inspect.
